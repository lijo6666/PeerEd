About the Centre

What is CCHS?

The Centre for Counselling & Health Services (CCHS) at CHRIST (Deemed to be University) is the central student wellness unit designed to foster the emotional, psychological, and physical well-being of the campus community.

Operating under the Director of Student Services, the Centre integrates professional clinical counselling, preventative mental health initiatives, and active student-led peer support networks with full-fledged campus primary healthcare clinics.

Our mission is "Preparing Students for Life" by cultivating self-awareness, personal growth, medical readiness, and a compassionate social fabric that supports every student throughout their academic journey.

Our core objective is to create a structured student well-being framework that works collaboratively with professional counsellors, medical wings, and peer networks.

Health & Medical Services

CCHS operates primary health wings across all blocks, staffed by a qualified Medical Officer to ensure health security.

The Happiness Hub

Happiness Hub – In Pursuit of Happiness Quotient

The Happiness Hub aims to build an emotionally resilient and happy campus by integrating structured well-being practices, theme-based engagements, and reflective daily routines. The initiative focuses on nurturing emotional balance, fostering meaningful interactions, and promoting a supportive environment where students and staff can grow, connect, and thrive. Through daily reflective habits and community-based engagements, Happiness Hub contributes to creating a positive campus culture rooted in awareness, wellness, and purposeful living.

Glimpses of Happiness Hub – CCHS

Explore our daily wellness therapies, group circles, and reflective activities designed to release stress and ground your emotions.

Mindful Breathing

A practice of mindful breathing that helps reduce stress, calm the mind, and improve overall focus.

Smile Therapy

Water Therapy

Uses the calming presence of water—through mindful drinking or interaction—to refresh the mind and relax the body.

Aroma Therapy

Uses scents like lavender or citrus to support relaxation and calmness.

Art Therapy

Nurtures creativity, expression, and emotional healing through colours, art, and craft.

Sound Therapy

Writing Therapy

Supporters

Offering non-judgmental active listening ears, comfort, and positive validation to fellow students.

Leaders

Ambassadors

Serving as vocal representatives raising awareness regarding psychological counseling resources.

Influencers

Creating a supportive, friendly culture that breaks the stigma surrounding emotional wellness.
